\begin{abstract}
In this lecture we will see the importance of studying QCD at finite baryon density. For this we will see how to add a chemical potential on the lattice and how this breaks the gamma 5 hermiticity of the fermion determinant. This will lead to a major open problem for lattice QCD at finite density - numerical sign problem. We will then discuss the implications of this problem.
\end{abstract}
